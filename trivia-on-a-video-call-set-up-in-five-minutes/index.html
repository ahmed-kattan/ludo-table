Trivia on a video call: set up in five minutes

Ahmed Moaz · 8 November 2026

A video call and a quiz go well together. You do not need any extra equipment, and it takes about five minutes to set up.

What you need

A video call, a link, and a device each. The quiz is Trivia Tavern, which takes two to eight players, so keep the group to eight or fewer.

The steps

  1. The host opens a table. Pick Trivia Tavern and create a table. It shows in the list of open tables unless the host taps the padlock to make it invite-only.
  2. Share the link. Post it in the call chat. A five-character code also works.
  3. Everyone joins. Guests need only a name. Nothing to install.
  4. Choose the categories. There are nine, plus a mixed setting.
  5. Start. Everyone answers on their own screen, so nobody needs to share theirs.

How long it lasts

In Boardit's table log, the middle Trivia Tavern game between 13 September and 30 October 2026 took ten minutes. Two or three games make a good call.

Tips for the call

  1. Mute during questions. It stops people calling out the answer.
  2. Unmute for results. The fun part is the argument over the number question, where the closest answer wins.
  3. Keep a spare device handy. If someone's phone fails, they can rejoin on another.

What can go wrong

Someone cannot hear the call, someone joins the wrong table, someone is on a slow connection. Each has a quick fix. Post the link again, check the table is the one you opened, and look at the signal at the top of the table. Green means fine, yellow means slow, red means they have dropped out, and their seat is held while they reconnect.

Where to start

You can use Boardit's Trivia Tavern, which is free. A connection signal at every table shows green, yellow or red, and your seat is held if you drop out.

The final bet on a laggy call

The final bet gives each player 20 seconds to place their stake, and the host can switch it on. On a call where someone's connection is slow, that is a fair amount of time, since the bet is a choice from four options: nothing, a quarter, half or all of the score.

The short version

One link, one host, ten minutes a game. That is all it takes to turn a video call into a quiz night.

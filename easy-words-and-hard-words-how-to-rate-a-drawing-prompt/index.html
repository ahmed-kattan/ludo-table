Easy words and hard words: how to rate a drawing prompt

Ahmed Moaz · 12 November 2026

A drawing game is only as good as its words. A word that is too easy gives nothing to enjoy, and one that is too hard produces blank faces. This is how I rate them before I add words to a list.

What makes a word easy to draw

  1. It has one clear shape. A sun, a house, a fish.
  2. It is a thing, not an idea. You can draw a chair; you cannot draw justice.
  3. It is seen from one side. A side view is enough.
  4. Few people would call it anything else. A guess has to be the same word.

What makes a word hard

  1. It is abstract. Feelings and ideas need a scene.
  2. It is a compound. Two things drawn in one picture.
  3. It has no outline. Weather, smells, sounds.
  4. It has a near twin. A word with a close synonym may produce the wrong guess, and in Draw and Guess a guess one letter wrong shows only to the guesser as close.

A three-tier test

I score each word out of three on the points above. Words that score three are easy, two are medium and one or none are hard. Then I put about half the list as easy, a third as medium and the rest as hard. A table can always draw an easy one when it has had enough of hard ones.

The drawer's choice

The drawer chooses one of a few words, between two and five. A mix of difficulties gives them a real choice. A drawer who gets five hard words will pick the least hard.

Adding your own words

The host can type a list. Words are separated by commas, each from 1 to 32 characters. Duplicates are dropped, ignoring case. The players never see the list.

A test run

Try the list yourself with a timer. If you cannot draw a word in 40 seconds, it is probably too hard for most tables.

The bank by group

The built-in words are sorted into groups, and the sizes show where the easy words are: vehicles has 60 words, sports and games 64 and brands 71. A group of everyday things is the one to build your own list from.

The scale in a sentence

One shape, one side, one word.

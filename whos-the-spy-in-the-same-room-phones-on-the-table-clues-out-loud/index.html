Who's the Spy in the same room: phones on the table, clues out loud

Ahmed Moaz · 11 November 2026

Who's the Spy has a mode for people who are in the same room. Everyone uses their own phone, but the clues are said out loud. It plays differently from the online game, and it is the one I would choose for a party.

How it works

You open a table and choose Same room. Everyone joins from their own phone, as in the online game, and the game deals each player the secret word, or the category for the spy. Nobody types a clue. You say it.

What changes

  1. Faces matter. You can see who hesitates.
  2. Clues are faster. Speaking takes less time than typing.
  3. No typing limit. Spoken clues are not typed, though a long one gives the word away.

The rules that stay

The spy knows only the category. A tie goes to a runoff between the tied players. Points are 1 for voting for a spy, 1 for a spy nobody votes for, and 1 more for a lone spy who names the word.

What you need

Phones, one each, and a table.

Tips for a room

  1. Put the phones face down when the word has been read.
  2. Go round in order so everyone gives a clue.
  3. Keep a poker face. A spy who looks worried is easy to catch.

Where to play

Boardit's Who's the Spy offers Online, Same room and One phone modes, free in the browser. It is the imposter game by another name.

When to choose it

If there are more than eight of you, use One phone, which takes up to twelve. If you are in different places, use Online. If you are around a table with phones, Same room is the one I would pick.

A party setup

Put the phones in the middle of the table until the host says go, pick up the one that is yours, read your word privately, then put it face down again. Go round the table once for clues. If the group is large and loud, use a talking object so only one person speaks at a time.

How many must ask

Calling the vote in the same room works the same way as online. After two laps of clues, at a table of eight, five of the eight players must ask for the vote before it opens, since the rule is more than half of the active players.

The mode in one line

Phones in hand, voices up.
